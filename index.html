It then places every live account on that path in real time and turns the gap into a ranked, owner-assigned action plan for forward deployed engineering and customer success teams.

HOW IT WORKS

01

Learn the winning shape

From history, plot how usage evolved for accounts that renewed or expanded versus those that fell short. That is the benchmark band.

02

Find the early signals

Measure which usage signals actually separated winners from losers, and at which quarter. Earlier and sharper is more useful.

03

Place every live account

Score each current account against the winning band at its own quarter, and rank the book by risk, size, and time to renewal.

04

Hand over an action plan

For each off-track account, produce a plan: the areas behind target, who owns each, and the number to move before renewal.

How the Agent Learns the Path

The time frame is the look-back horizon the agent learns over - 12, 24, or 36 months - not a contract term. This showcase is look-back only: it places each account on the learned path. Forward forecasting (projecting the path ahead) is the natural next step, not built here.

Pick a time frame and a Trajectory Signal. The green band is the range for accounts that renewed; the red band is the range for accounts that fell short. The moment they separate is the moment this signal can tell a winner from a loser.

Each cell scores one signal at one quarter. Read it as separation power: 0.50 means the signal tells you nothing yet, 1.00 means it perfectly splits winners from losers. Greener is better.

CS/FDE Trajectory Signal Library Definitions

The signals the agent weighs to see where an account is heading - toward renewal and expansion, or toward churn - a quarter or more before the renewal conversation.

Assumptions and how to read the results

This is an exploration prototype running on synthetic (invented) data. Its job is to prove the logic before real data is gathered, not to make live renewal decisions.

What the colors mean

At risk - act now Needs attention On track to renew

A single signal inside an account's plan is colored by how far it sits outside the winning range: green within, amber modestly outside, and red clearly off target.

An account's overall band is deliberately not set by any single signal - one wobbly metric should not condemn a whole account. Accounts are ranked by total gap. So an account can be green overall while still showing a red signal or two to work on in its plan.
